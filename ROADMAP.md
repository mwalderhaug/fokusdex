# Roadmap

Agreed with Jazz on 10 Nov 2026. Step 1 is done; steps 2 and 3 are next.

## Step 1 – foundation (done)
Base stats, IVs, natures, EVs from focus minutes, level-up moves (Black 2/White 2), stat card with hexagon and evolution path,
wild levels that follow the team, 3 coins per minute, and a choice of which save to keep when connecting sync.

## Step 2 – trainer battles
- A "Done for today" button unlocks the trainer ladder. Battles can be taken later, and there is no cap on how many per day.
- Turn-based single battles (double battles may come later): pick a move, switch, use items. Type chart, STAB, crits, accuracy,
  priority, and the main status moves (stat stages, sleep, poison, burn, paralysis).
- Gen 5 layout: own Pokémon bottom left with animated back sprite, opponent top right, HP boxes, EXP bar, text box.
  Own artwork for arena, platforms and move animations (per type and physical/special). Standard arena for normal trainers.
- Every fifth trainer is a boss: a well-known Gym Leader/Elite/Champion with their original team, an own background per boss,
  and a VS intro where the music switches from normal to boss music. After beating a boss, pick one Pokémon from their team.
- Trainers start weak and get stronger, more balanced and closer to the player's level. Difficulty keeps rising.
- Rewards (coins, items) scale with today's focus time: x0.5 with no study up to x2.0 at five hours. XP only x0.8–1.2.
  Rewards also grow the further up the ladder you are. The ladder persists across days; losing ends battles for the day.
- Show the record (highest rung reached) so there is something to beat.
- After a battle or a loss: an overview screen in Hall of Fame style with the team, their stats and the rewards.
- Sounds: own battle music and boss theme, three distinct hit sounds (super effective, normal, not very effective).
  Setting "Choose your own music": local mp3 files for battle and boss, stored on the device only (never in the repo).

## Step 3 – items
- Random item finds during sessions, and wild Pokémon that sometimes hold an item.
- Held items with a unique use: Lucky Egg, Amulet Coin, Leftovers, type boosters, Exp. Share.
- Battle items: Potion, Revive. Rare items: Master Ball, Shiny Charm.
